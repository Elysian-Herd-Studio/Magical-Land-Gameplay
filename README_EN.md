# Magical Land Gameplay

English | [简体中文](README.md)

</div>

Magical Land Gameplay is an addon for [Magical Land](https://github.com/Elysian-Herd-Studio/Magical-Land), bringing pony tribe abilities and achievements to Minecraft.

## Gameplay

### Tribe abilities

- **Unicorns**: telekinetic projection, self-levitation, and remotely controlled tools for guarding or gathering.
- **Pegasi**: hovering, gliding, loops and rolls, with wing-assisted landings and collision rebounds.
- **Earth ponies**: tremor sense to detect nearby ground activity.

Choose a race when you first enter a world. Changing race normally requires a potion. Servers can allow free changes and decide whether horns and wings must match your race. See [race selection](docs/races.md) and the [ability guides](docs/README.md#三族能力).

### Achievement system

Unlock achievements as you explore and interact, and view them in Minecraft's advancement screen. See the [achievement guide](docs/achievements.md) for available achievements and their requirements.

## Installation

For Minecraft **1.20.1 / Fabric**. Install the following on both client and server:

- Fabric Loader 0.19.1 or newer, and Fabric API.
- Magical Land Gameplay `0.3.4`.
- Magical Land `>=0.3.6 <0.4.0`, and GeckoLib 4.7 or newer.

Upgrade the client and server together. When upgrading from the old combined mod, remove it before installing Magical Land and Gameplay.

Mod Menu is an optional client-side addition that opens the gameplay settings page. The Magical Land main mod can also be used on its own.

## Getting started

- Hold **R** to open the ability wheel, select an ability and release.
- Press **V** to use the selected ability. Bindings can be changed in Minecraft's controls settings.
- As a pegasus, double-tap Jump to take off or land; press **Ctrl** while flying to switch between hovering and gliding.
- With Mod Menu installed, open “Mods → Magical Land: Gameplay → Configure” to adjust ability settings, choose a race or view server rules.

Ability controls and rules are collected in the [documentation index](docs/README.md).
